Lockdown Range

Full hardening labs in your browser, no virtual machine needed: Windows 11 and Windows Server, Ubuntu and Linux Mint, and a Packet Tracer-style Cisco topology. Sign in, read the scenario brief, answer the investigation questions, fix the machine, and watch a live Progress Check.

Unofficial practice; not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Canonical, Linux Mint, or Cisco. Simulated machines only: nothing touches your computer, and progress stays in your browser.
