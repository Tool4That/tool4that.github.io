Click squares to add hits · drag to paint · Shift-click or right-click for a soft hit · switch patterns while playing and the change lands on the next bar.

Every kit is 16 sounds made right here in your browser, free to use in anything you make. Load kit puts them on the pads with a starter beat at the style’s tempo and swing; Undo brings back what you had.

Click to play from a spot · drag to select (then chop tools work inside it) · double-click to add a chop · press Enter while playing to drop a chop on the beat · drag a yellow marker to move it · right-click a marker to remove it · scroll to zoom, Shift+scroll to move.

Drag a sound onto a deck · drag the platter in circles or drag the zoomed wave to scratch · Jump back repeats a word or beat · Double up plays two copies one beat apart, so you can cut between them.

Welcome to Chop Lab

  1. Hear the starter beat. Press Space or Play. Click squares in the grid to add or remove hits.
  2. Play the pads with your keyboard: 1234 · QWER · ASDF · ZXCV. Turn on Record hits to finger-drum straight into the pattern.
  3. Pick a genre. The Genres tab holds 63 kits across 17 families, from boom bap, trap, and house to jazz, reggae, country, and metal. Press Hear it, then Load kit to put its sounds and a starter beat on the pads.
  4. Bring your own sounds. Drop MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, or FLAC files anywhere, or record with the mic. Drag any sound onto a pad or a deck.
  5. Chop. Open a sound in the Chop tab, chop it evenly, on hits, or by beats (or tap Enter while it plays), then press Send chops to pads.
  6. Screw. Slide Screw down to slow and deepen everything together. For a whole track, pick it in Sounds and press Screw for a chopped & screwed version.
  7. DJ. In Decks, scratch on the platter, sync the tempo, set hot cues, loop, and use Jump back and Double up for screw-style repeats.
  8. Record and share. Rec captures everything you hear into Your sounds. Chop it again, or press WAV to save a file.
