Cybersecurity

Two ways in. Learning builds the concepts, from your first command to confident defender. Practice is hands-on reps: Net Drills for subnetting and Cisco IOS, Lockdown Drills for hardening Windows, Linux, FreeBSD, and Cisco IOS, and Lockdown Range for full hardening labs in your browser.

Unofficial practice; not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Canonical, Linux Mint, the FreeBSD Project, or Cisco. Defensive skills only: practice on machines you own or are authorized to use. Progress stays in your browser.

Learning

Build the concepts and vocabulary, from your first terminal command to confident defender. New to cybersecurity? Start with Defense Ladder, the course. Know what you need? The Study Map finds any concept as you type and shows where to learn and practice it.

  • Defense Ladder

    A free, four-grade cybersecurity course, Freshman to Senior, built on Bloom's taxonomy: 54 units across Windows, Linux, networking, security foundations, defense operations, and web and cloud. Practice

Hands-on reps once you know the ideas. Net Drills are timed networking drills. Lockdown Drills walk you through hardening each system step by step. Lockdown Range drops you into a full simulated machine to harden on your own, with a live Progress Check.
