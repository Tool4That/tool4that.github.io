Cybersecurity

Two ways in: Learning builds the concepts from your first command to confident defender, and Practice is hands-on reps — Net Drills for subnetting and Cisco IOS, Lockdown Drills for hardening Windows, Linux, FreeBSD, and Cisco IOS, and Lockdown Range for full hardening labs that run in your browser.

Unofficial practice; not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Canonical, Linux Mint, the FreeBSD Project, or Cisco. Defensive skills only: practice on machines you own or are authorized to use. Progress stays in your browser.

Learning

Build the concepts and vocabulary, from your first terminal command to confident defender. Start here if cybersecurity is new, and use the Concept Index to find where to learn and practice any idea.

  • Defense Ladder

    From first command to defender, one grade at a time.

    A four-grade cybersecurity learning ladder, Freshman to Senior, built on Bloom's taxonomy and aligned with the NICE Framework and CIS Controls. Concepts first, then quizzes and config-reading drills, then a hardening checklist and study plan you build yourself. Everything stays in your browser.
