PFAT Personal Finance Advisor Tool

See where your money goes. Everything stays on this device.

to

Copy transactions from your bank's website, a statement PDF, or a spreadsheet and paste them here. One transaction per line works best: a date, a description, and an amount. Column-style pastes (tab-separated) and OFX/QIF text are recognized too.

Files are read in your browser and never uploaded. Bank, card and investment statements all work: PDFs use pdf.js (fetched from cdnjs), and screenshots or scanned PDFs use Tesseract, the open-source OCR engine (fetched once from jsDelivr, with your OK first).

PFAT, the Personal Finance Advisor Tool, runs entirely in your browser. No account, no server, no tracking.
