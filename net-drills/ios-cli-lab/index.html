      Type ? for help and Tab to finish a word. Abbreviations work: conf t, int g0/0.10, int range f0/1 - 4, sh run, copy run start. Filters work on show commands: sh run | section line vty, sh run | include access-list, sh ip int br | include Vlan. PCs use ipconfig /renew for DHCP. The simulator covers the commands the missions and the Defense Ladder's Cisco units use; anything else gets an IOS-style error. Output follows classic IOS 15 on a 2960 switch and a 2911 router, as in Packet Tracer; the tips say where IOS XE differs.
      

      What the simulator covers
      Device basics; passwords, SSH, and AAA with local logins; VLANs, trunks, VTP, and spanning tree; port security; router-on-a-stick; DHCP, DHCP snooping, and ARP inspection; static routes; standard and extended ACLs, numbered and named, on interfaces and VTY lines, with the ICMP unreachables and log messages their drops produce; login banners; CDP and LLDP neighbors; SPAN sessions; logging, NTP, SNMP, and web-server settings; and OSPF at the configuration level. Show commands take the | include, | exclude, | begin, and | section filters.
      

      Unofficial practice; not affiliated with or endorsed by Cisco. The router, switch, and PCs are simulated; output is modeled on Cisco IOS. Completed missions are remembered in this browser only.
