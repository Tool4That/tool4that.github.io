Net Drills

Four networking drills: subnetting and IPv6, Cisco IOS configuration, config audits, and troubleshooting from real device output. They pair well with Cisco networking courses such as the NetAcad Network Technician path.

Unofficial practice; not affiliated with or endorsed by Cisco. Progress stays in your browser.

  • Subnet Sprint

    Calculate it. Then faster.

    Slide the mask in the sandbox and watch every value react, then work out network, broadcast, and host ranges against the clock. Includes VLSM planning and IPv6 drills.

    Configure it. Then prove it.
