      Common questions

      What percent should I put in the TSP to max out?

      It depends on your base pay, any special or incentive pay, what you've already put in this year, and when a change takes effect. Add your LES in step 1 and the calculator gives the whole percentage for each myPay box, so you reach the limit in your last December paycheck.

      Will maxing out early cost me the match?

      Yes. Once you reach the yearly limit, your contributions stop, and the BRS match stops with them for the rest of the year. Spreading contributions so the limit lands in your last paycheck keeps every dollar of match.

      What if my percentages are a little too high?

      DFAS stops your last deduction exactly at the limit, and the rest stays in that paycheck. That's why this calculator rounds up, as long as the last paycheck still covers 5% of base pay so the match stays whole.

      Do special, incentive, and bonus pays count?

      Yes. You can contribute a percentage of each as long as you also contribute from base pay, and they count toward the same yearly limit. The match is based on base pay only.

      When does a myPay change take effect?

      Usually within one or two pay periods; myPay shows the effective date when you submit. Confirm the new percentages and deduction on your next LES.

      Roth or Traditional TSP?

      Roth is taxed now, and qualified withdrawals, growth included, are tax-free later. Traditional lowers your taxable pay now and is taxed when you withdraw. Both count toward the same limit, and the match always goes to Traditional.

      Is my LES uploaded or saved?

      No. It's read only in your browser and never uploaded. Only pay numbers are kept, and only while Remember is on; never your name, SSN, or account numbers.

      Does it work for federal civilians?

      The math handles FERS (Advanced, then Profile), but civilians can elect a whole-dollar amount per paycheck instead of a percentage. TSP.gov's How much can I contribute? calculator gives that amount directly.

      Is this an official DoD tool?

      No. It's an independent calculator, not affiliated with or endorsed by DFAS, the Federal Retirement Thrift Investment Board, or the Department of Defense. Always check the numbers against your LES and myPay.
